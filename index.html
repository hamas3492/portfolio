About Me

I'm Hamas Ahmed, a BS Artificial Intelligence student at University of Sindh, Jamshoro — and a distribution professional who has run his own business for three years.

My story runs on three tracks at once. By day I study AI: programming fundamentals, problem-solving, and how intelligent systems actually work. In the market I handle order booking, deliveries, payments and customer relationships myself. And when the day ends, I edit — lyric videos and emotional cuts that have pulled 79.4K views on a single edit.

I also built Hemix AI, write for my Urdu channel Silent Reflections, and speak four languages — Sindhi, Urdu, English and Punjabi. Different worlds, one habit: I learn by doing.
